Registrar

The Office of the Registrar is the primary source for all information regarding attendance, academic courses, graduation, diplomas, transcripts, and other related topics.

Your Academic Records and Registration Hub

The Office of the Registrar at University of the Cumberlands is your primary resource for managing academic records and processes. Whether you need to register for classes, request transcripts, apply for graduation, or verify enrollment, our dedicated team is here to assist you. We provide comprehensive support to ensure your academic journey is smooth and well-organized. Access essential forms, deadlines, and information through our office to stay on track with your educational goals.

The links on this page will help you find answers to various questions, but if you need to visit us in person, we are located on the first floor of the Gatliff Building. You may also email us at registrar [at] ucumberlands.edu (registrar[at]ucumberlands[dot]edu) or call our office at 606.539.4316.

Ordering Transcripts

Need a transcript? Let us know. We'll handle it.

Important Info for Ordering a Transcript

The University of the Cumberlands has partnered with Parchment Transcript Services to expedite the processing of official paper and electronic transcripts in PDF format. 

  • As our agent, Parchment will collect all fees and allow you to track and receive confirmation and delivery receipt emails of your orders.
  • Notifications will be sent to your email directly from Parchment indicating the progress of your request for an electronic transcript. 
  • An official transcript is a copy of a student's entire academic record from the University of the Cumberlands. 
  • When ordering transcripts through Parchment you must create a username and password exclusive to Parchment. The Parchment site does not utilize your UC username and password information.
  • Students who have officially changed their name, please provide the name used while enrolled at the University of the Cumberlands.
  • Please check to make sure your grades are posted on your UC student portal before ordering your transcript.
  • All transcript orders require a major credit or debit card (Visa, MasterCard, Discover, or American Express) to complete the transaction.

Transcript Fees
Digital Transcript fee is $ 10.00
Paper Transcript fee is $ 12.50

*Transcript fees may include applicable sales tax.

Additional Expedited Delivery option through FedEx and fees are listed on the Parchment website during the ordering process.

Processing time
Standard orders are processed within 24 hours from receipt of the order, except orders placed on weekends and holidays. Delivery time is not included in processing time, and varies by delivery service and local area.

Current Students: 

  • Log in to MyUC (Workday)
  • Go to Other Academic Links
  • Choose Generate Unofficial Transcript 
    (OR)
  • In the search bar, type "Generate"
  • Select Generate Unofficial Transcript
  • Check the Confirm box and choose OK

You can wait for your transcript to generate or can select "notify me later" and your transcript will populate. Once populated you can download the transcript. 

Former Students: 

Please email Registrar [at] ucumberlands.edu (Registrar[at]ucumberlands[dot]edu) to request your Unofficial Transcript. Be sure to include your full name, any name used while attending UC, your date of birth, and a copy of a government-issued photo ID. Your unofficial transcript will be emailed to you within one business day.

Degree Completion Process

University of the Cumberlands approves the conferring of degrees six times per year. These conferral dates represent confirmation that all degree requirements have been met by that date. The Official conferral date is posted to the student’s academic transcript and is the date printed on the diploma. 

Diplomas are produced and mailed to students after all final grades are reviewed and it has been confirmed that all degree requirements have been met. The graduation confirmation process takes several weeks to complete. Students will receive a confirmation email of Congratulations once the process is complete. At this time the official degree date will be posted on the students’ transcript and available to request. 

How to Apply for Degree Completion

Students must submit the application for degree completion in MyUC within two semesters of their anticipated program completion date. The application must be submitted no later than the semester prior to the intended conferral date. 

If a student completes all degree requirements without submitting an application, the University may proceed with degree conferral. Students will be notified through their UC email account and have one (1) week to request a delay; otherwise, the degree will be automatically conferred.

Participation in the commencement ceremony is optional and separate from degree conferral.

  1. In MyUC, select the Academics application.
  2. Go to Academic Links and then select Apply for Program Completion/Graduation 
  3. Select Apply, Confirm the details, and Submit your application.
  4. Click Done once you have completed the application.
  • Please refer to the Academic Calendar for the correct Degree Conferral date to be used when applying.

In-School Deferment Request:

To have an in-school deferment form completed, please submit your request through MyUC (Workday) by following the steps below:

  1. Log in to MyUC (Workday)
  2. Go to Other Academic Links
  3. Select "In-School Deferment Request"
  4. Complete the request form
  5. Upload your In-School Deferment Form with the student section completed and signed

Please note that requests cannot be processed without the completed and signed form.
