# Provisional official webpage source set

Captured October 9, 2026 from **public official University of the Cumberlands web pages**.
Their HTML bodies were preserved in `official_web_sources/` and exported without
intentional paraphrasing using `scripts/build_official_snapshots.py`. The PDFs in
`raw_pdfs/` are **project-created snapshots**, not PDFs published or approved by UC.
PDF page numbers refer to the snapshot pagination. They can change if you rerun
the exporter with other fonts/layout settings.

The university website links to the 2026–2027 Graduate Catalog, but that catalog
host returned HTTP 403 from our environment. The linked Student Handbook required
account access. Accordingly, **this set remains provisional until the instructor
approves these exports or supplies the official catalog/handbook PDFs**.

The benchmark's 25 answerable questions were written from the archived official
text and manually assigned page references, and 5 unanswerable questions are
personalized/future facts not established by the snapshots. Review the original
webpages for changes before making decisions. These snapshots are educational
demonstration sources and do not make the assistant an official advising service.
